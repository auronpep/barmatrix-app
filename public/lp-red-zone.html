▌ What A Red Zone Is · 01

Not a weak subject. A weak pattern.

A Red Zone isn't "you're bad at Evidence." It's far more precise: it's a specific trap shape — a Stale Rule, a Wrong Exception — that you fall for at a rate well above the focus-group base. The same shape may cost you points in three different subjects.

Subject-level scores hide this. You can be "fine at Contracts" overall and still bleed points on every Wrong-Timing distractor inside it.

A Red Zone makes the invisible pattern visible — and a visible pattern is a fixable one. That's the whole premise.
